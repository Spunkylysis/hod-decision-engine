Projection basis iHow pts_mid is computed.
🏹 FV-based: early season — uses scouting grade × reach probability.
🎰 Blend: mid-season — weighted mix of FV and actual pace.
No badge: late season — full season pace extrapolation.

Format flags iThis league's scoring rewards walks (BB) and strikeouts (K). These flags adjust FV projections up/down based on a player's BB% and K% relative to league thresholds. Applied before the Pareto calculation.
